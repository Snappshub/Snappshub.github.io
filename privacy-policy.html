Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This page explains what information may be handled when you visit SN Apps Hub, a publisher-linked software catalogue. The site does not provide visitor accounts or a public submission form.

1. Information handled by this website

The public catalogue page does not ask visitors to submit their name, phone number, password, or account details. If you contact the site using the Email support link, your email application opens and the information you choose to send is handled through your email provider.

The website is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider and network infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser, device, and request metadata, as part of delivering and protecting the site. SN Apps Hub does not control the provider's independent logs or retention practices.

2. Catalogue service and browser storage

The website requests catalogue data from a public Supabase endpoint and falls back to the published apps-data.json file if that service is unavailable. These requests may expose normal connection metadata to the service provider. The public page source also contains a browser local-storage helper for a catalogue snapshot; it is not used for visitor sign-in, passwords, or payment details.

3. Third-party resources and links

The site loads interface resources such as Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS CDN, and may request publisher website icons from Google's favicon service. App cards link to external software publishers and repositories. The site also links to Telegram and DMCA services. When you load or visit those services, their operators may collect information under their own privacy policies. Review those policies before using an external service.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

No first-party advertising, analytics tag, or cookie-setting script was identified in the public website source reviewed for this policy. This describes the inspected source at the effective date; hosting providers or third-party resources may operate their own technical storage or logging. If advertising or analytics services are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled.

5. Downloads and safety

SN Apps Hub primarily links to publisher sources and does not claim that every external file is independently scanned or verified. When you follow a download link, you leave this site and become subject to the destination publisher's terms and privacy practices. Check the publisher, device compatibility, release details and integrity information before installing software.

6. Children's privacy

The site is a general software catalogue and does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the site by email, contact the site using the Email support link so the matter can be reviewed.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website's features or service providers change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.
